WHY CODE-307

Built around the way you work.

Code-307 LLC is a Wheatland, Wyoming-based emergency vehicle lighting and upfitting company serving customers across Wyoming, Nebraska, and Colorado. We specialize in practical, professional vehicle solutions designed around the people who depend on their vehicles every day.

From emergency response and command vehicles to work trucks, storm-chasing rigs, semis, and custom applications, we take the time to understand what your vehicle needs to do and build the equipment around that mission.

Our goal is simple: provide clean, dependable installations and help each customer create a vehicle that is functional, professional, and ready to perform when it matters most.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Questions? We've got answers.

Here are a few common questions about Code-307 and our vehicle lighting and upfitting services.

What types of vehicles does Code-307 upfit?

We work with fire and rescue vehicles, emergency command vehicles, storm-chasing vehicles, work trucks, semis, and custom vehicle applications.

What areas does Code-307 serve?

We're based in Wheatland, Wyoming and serve customers throughout Wyoming, Nebraska, and Colorado.

What brands do you install?

We work with Havis, STL, Whelen, SoundOff Signal, Nilight, and other equipment manufacturers depending on the customer's needs.

Can Code-307 install equipment I already have?

Customer-supplied equipment may be considered depending on the equipment, vehicle, and project. Contact us with the details and we'll discuss your options.

Do I have to know exactly what equipment I want?

No. Tell us what the vehicle is used for and what you're trying to accomplish, and we can start the conversation from there.

Can I get a quote before bringing you my vehicle?

Absolutely. Use the Request a Quote form and provide as much information as possible about your vehicle and project.
