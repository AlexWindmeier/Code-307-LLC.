WHO WE ARE

Experience you can count on.

Code-307 LLC is a Wheatland, Wyoming-based emergency vehicle lighting and upfitting company serving customers across Wyoming, Nebraska, and Colorado.

With 10+ years of experience in the emergency services industry, we understand that emergency vehicles are tools people depend on when seconds matter. We focus on quality equipment, clean installations, and practical setups designed around the mission.

Whether the project is a single lighting upgrade or a larger vehicle upfit, our goal is to make the finished setup functional, organized, and matched to the way the vehicle will actually be used.
